# Contributing

Contributing here means sharing what you know or what you made.

## Share a score or an exercise

1. Open a new discussion in [Show and tell](../../discussions/categories/show-and-tell). The form asks what the piece is, its level and how you made it.
2. Drag the `.musicxml` or `.mxl` file into the text box. GitHub attaches it to the post.
3. Check that it imports in midipiano.app (Import tab) before posting, so others can use it right away.
4. Only your own work or public domain music. Copyrighted songs, arrangements, editions or scans of method books are removed.

Files that many people find useful may be added to a `community/` folder in this repository, with your name and a link to your post. We will ask you first.

## Report how your keyboard works

Open a discussion in [MIDI keyboards and lights](../../discussions/categories/midi-keyboards-and-lights). Model, connection (USB or Bluetooth), the device and browser, whether notes arrive and whether the key lights work. One keyboard per post, so people can find it.

## Fix a translation

Open a discussion in [Translations](../../discussions/categories/translations) with the language, where the text appears in the app, the current text and the text you propose.

## Report a bug

Use the [bug report form](../../issues/new/choose). Fill in the device, the browser and the keyboard: without them most reports cannot be reproduced.

## Propose an idea

Open a discussion in [Ideas](../../discussions/categories/ideas). Explain what you are trying to do and where the app gets in the way. Ideas are read, but there is no promise about what gets built or when.

## Tone

Plain and friendly. Many people here are learning the piano as adults and English is not everyone's first language. Write in English if you can, so that more people can follow. If you cannot, write in your language and someone will help.
