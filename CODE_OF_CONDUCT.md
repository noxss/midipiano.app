# Code of conduct

This is a small community of people who practice the piano. Keep it pleasant.

- Be kind. Many people here are beginners, and adult beginners in particular tend to be hard on themselves. Nobody needs help with that.
- Discuss the music, the keyboard, the app. Not the person.
- No harassment, no insults, no discrimination, no unwanted personal remarks. No spam, no advertising.
- Do not post personal data, yours or anyone else's. A keyboard model is useful, an email address is not.
- Respect other people's work. Only share files you are allowed to share, and credit the author.

Posts that break these rules are removed and repeat offenders are blocked. If something bothers you and you would rather not say it in public, write to contact@midipiano.app.
