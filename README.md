[midipiano.app](https://midipiano.app) is a web app for practicing piano with a MIDI keyboard. This is where its users meet.

## Exercises

The app's built-in exercises as MusicXML files, in [`exercises/`](exercises/), with an index by level. They are the exact scores the app shows. Open one in a notation editor, or give it to your AI together with the prompt the app's Import tab builds for you, and make your own version: transposed, longer, simpler. Then import the result from the Import tab.

## Discussions

- **Q&A**: help with a keyboard, the lights, a browser or a score that does not import.
- **Ideas and suggestions**: what you would like the app to do, or an exercise you miss in the catalog.
- **Show and tell**: scores and exercises you made.
- **Translations**: fixes for the app's texts in your language.
- **General**: everything else, including how your keyboard works with the app.
